## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for the high-density, fast-paced commuter environment of Singapore. It balances high-throughput utility with restrained, civic elegance. The system prioritizes immediate glanceability under varying lighting conditions—from direct equatorial sunlight at outdoor bus stops to the stark fluorescent illumination of MRT concourses.

The design movement is **Civic Functionalism**: an evolution of Swiss rationalism tailored for mobile software. It discards playful ornamentation, skeuomorphism, and vibrant consumer-tech neons in favor of disciplined data hierarchy, typographic density, spatial precision, and high-legibility telemetry. 

Every UI element is tuned to reduce cognitive load during transit:
- **Instant Recognition:** Critical transit data (arrival countdowns, occupancy, vehicle deck types) takes absolute precedence over branding or decorative graphics.
- **Civic Restraint:** Calm, architectural slate and zinc neutrals create an unobtrusive backdrop that feels systematic, reliable, and institutional.
- **Physical Clarity:** High-contrast lineation and low-elevation surfaces mimic physical urban signage and physical wayfinding panels.

## Colors

The palette avoids saturated neon hues and tech-forward purples/magentas, anchoring itself in architectural neutrals and muted functional indicators. 

### Palette Application
- **Primary Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity off-white and cool zinc tones provide glare-resistant readability on commuter screens.
- **Primary Ink (`#0F172A` & `#1E293B`):** Deep slate provides near-black typographic contrast compliant with strict WCAG AAA guidelines for small metrics and arrival numbers.
- **Secondary Slate (`#475569`):** Subdued functional slate for intermediate metadata, structural borders (`#E2E8F0`), and secondary actions.
- **Neutral Rail (`#64748B`):** Structural neutral for inactive states, divider lines, and peripheral route details.

### Functional Transit Status Tokens
Occupancy and status cues rely on muted, earthy chromatic signals calibrated to standard Singapore public transit datasets:
- **Available / Seats Available (SEA):** Muted Sage (`#3F6253` on light fill `#ECFDF5`). Communicates reassurance without electric brightness.
- **Standing Available (SDA):** Warm Ochre Amber (`#B45309` on light fill `#FFFBEB`). Pragmatic, high-visibility operational caution.
- **Limited Standing (LSD) / Arriving Now:** Muted Terracotta Brick (`#991B1B` on light fill `#FEF2F2`). Urgent, high-alert readability without harsh eye strain.
- **Vehicle Type Indicators:** Subtle Cool Slate fill (`#F1F5F9`) with `#334155` text for WAB (Wheelchair Accessible), SD (Single Deck), DD (Double Deck), and BD (Bendy).

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by Inter, deployed with tabular numbers (`tnum`) enabled across all numeric and telemetry surfaces. This prevents layout jitter during real-time countdown updates.

### Typographic Principles
- **Dense Data Presentation:** Strict line-height-to-font-size ratios guarantee that compact bus timing lists do not bleed into excessive vertical space.
- **Glance Metric System:** The `metric-eta` token is reserved specifically for the leading arrival minutes (e.g., `2m`, `Arr`, `14m`). It is optimized to align cleanly adjacent to the `metric-badge` indicators.
- **Upper-Case Badge Formats:** Metadata tags (`WAB`, `DD`, `SD`, `BD`) strictly utilize `label-sm` or `metric-badge` with uppercase transformation and positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) to ensure legibility inside 16px to 20px badge chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 4px base increment, biased toward compact, vertical information density. Commuters need multiple bus routes visible on viewport load without aggressive scrolling.

### Grid & Fluid Boundaries
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 430px):** Single-column stacked data feed. Screen margins fixed at `1rem` (16px), with list gutters at `0.75rem` (12px). Fixed top search/telemetry filter with edge-to-edge scrollable feed.
- **Tablet / Responsive Slate (600px – 1024px):** Split-view architecture. Left-hand master transit list fixed at 380px; remaining space dedicated to the live vector route map and schematic stop diagram.
- **Desktop Concierge (> 1024px):** Contained max-width layout (1200px) with 3-column triage: Active Line Selector (left), Live Station Arrival Board (center, 640px), and Real-time Incident & Capacity Matrix (right).

### Density Rules
Padding within cards and row items conforms strictly to `space-sm` (8px) vertically and `space-md` (12px) horizontally to maximize row counts per fold while maintaining a minimum 44px hit-target for all interactable cells.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids expressive drop shadows and high-blur glassmorphic treatments. Depth is achieved via **structural borders and planar surface layering**, ensuring maximum battery efficiency and fluid 120Hz frame rates across all mobile chipsets.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** Cool base stone `#F8FAFC`.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Row Surfaces):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` bound by a crisp 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
3. **Level 2 (Pinned Telemetry & Sticky Bars):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border-b `#CBD5E1` and an imperceptible ambient contact shadow (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04) 0px 2px 4px`).
4. **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Route Filters):** `#FFFFFF` bound by a 1px stroke (`#94A3B8`) paired with an anchored directional drop: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

Dividers between tabular items are crisp 1px strokes in `#F1F5F9` with zero offset.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is calibrated to `1` (Soft, 4px baseline). Transit cards, arrival chips, and control blocks use deliberate, modest radiuses rather than broad rounded curves or circular pills.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard Surfaces (Cards, Search Inputs, Panels):** `0.25rem` (4px). Provides an orderly, ledger-like architectural structure.
- **Containers & Action Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for sheet drawers, route summary hero cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Transit Telemetry Badges:** `0.25rem` (4px) with interior 2px padding for tight-fitting tag bounding.
- **Segmented Commuter Switches:** Inset 4px squircle toggles within an outer 6px container.

## Components

### Bus Timing & Arrival Cards
- **Structure:** Multi-slot inline row. Left-aligned bus service number (`display-lg-mobile` equivalent, bold slate `#0F172A`).
- **Arrival Triad:** Three successive arrival columns (Next, 2nd Bus, 3rd Bus). Each column houses:
  - Countdown time (`metric-eta`, e.g., `Arr`, `3m`, `12m`).
  - Occupancy Bar Indicator: 3px-tall indicator bar using status tokens (`#3F6253` for SEA, `#B45309` for SDA, `#991B1B` for LSD).
  - Vehicle Attributes: Monospaced uppercase sub-badges (`DD`, `WAB`) rendered in `metric-badge`.

### Badges & Pill Metadata
- **Occupancy Badges:**
  - `Seats Available (SEA)`: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#166534`, border `#BBF7D0`.
  - `Standing Available (SDA)`: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#854D0E`, border `#FDE68A`.
  - `Limited Standing (LSD)`: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`.
- **Accessibility & Deck Badges:** Solid `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` stroke `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`.

### Buttons & Quick Controls
- **Universal Icon Controls:** 40x40px or 32x32px square buttons with 4px border radius. 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, white surface, active state shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Primary Service Button:** Solid `#1E293B` background, white label, 0.25rem radius, zero shadow, high tactile press state (`#0F172A`).
- **Segmented Direction Selectors:** Compact dual-tab switch (e.g., "To Marina Centre" vs "To Woodlands") encased in a 1px slate frame with high contrast active state.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, focus ring 1px solid `#1E293B` with zero outer blur. Monospaced placeholder text support for direct 5-digit bus stop codes (e.g., `01012`, `64009`).

### Live Telemetry Indicators
- **Real-Time Pulse:** A static 6px solid dot using Sage (`#3F6253`) indicates active live GPS synchronization. If data is estimated/scheduled, the dot defaults to slate `#94A3B8`.
